Good to know

Before you get started

Do I need previous volunteer experience?

Not necessarily. Tell the centre about your interests and availability so a suitable opportunity can be discussed, if one is available.

Can a business or organisation partner with the centre?

You can submit a partnership enquiry. Any arrangement must be reviewed and confirmed by the organisation.

Are volunteer roles and event dates confirmed here?

No. This is a demo website and does not publish verified vacancies or event dates.
