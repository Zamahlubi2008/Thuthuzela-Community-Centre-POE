Privacy notice

How this educational website prototype handles information.

Demonstration website

This website is a student project prototype. The contact and enquiry forms perform browser-side validation only. They do not send, save or transmit the information entered.

Personal information

Please do not enter real sensitive or confidential information into these demonstration forms. If this website is developed for a real organisation, its authorised representatives must provide and approve a privacy notice explaining what information is collected, why it is used, how long it is kept and how people can exercise their rights under applicable privacy law.

External content

Some photographs and the map preview are loaded from external services. Those services may process technical information when a page loads. Their terms and privacy policies apply.

Note: This sample notice is for the project prototype and is not legal advice or an approved policy for a real organisation.
